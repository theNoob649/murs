Play Classic

Minecraft as it was in the summer of 2009 — free, in your browser, no account needed. Click inside the game to start.

Open in a new tab Read about Classic

What Is This?

To celebrate Minecraft's 10th anniversary in May 2019, Mojang put the original Classic build back online. It's version 0.0.23a_01, the last build of Classic's multiplayer test from July 2009, and it runs exactly like it did back then — including the bugs.

There's no survival, no crafting, and no day-night cycle. It's pure creative mode: a small island surrounded by a bedrock sea, and 32 blocks to build with, most of them colored wool (which was still called “cloth”).

Play With Friends

Classic has multiplayer built in. When you start a world, you're the host, and you can invite up to 9 friends by sending them your world's link. To get it, use Open in a new tab above, then copy the address from that tab.

Doesn't Work?

Classic needs a keyboard and mouse, so it won't really work on phones or tablets. If the game won't take your mouse, click inside it once, or use Open in a new tab.
