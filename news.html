News & Announcements

The big Minecraft announcements that aren't a game drop yet. Last updated November 15, 2026.

Coming Up

November 15, 2026 — the fourth drop gets its name: Deep Freeze. Mojang revealed the name of the Ice Caves drop during MC Championship: Absolute Zero. See what's in it.

December 2026 — Deep Freeze releases. Java 26.4 / Bedrock 26.70. No exact date yet.

Minecraft Live — September 26, 2026

The Sift: a fourth dimension. The headline reveal. The Sift joins the Overworld, the Nether, and the End as Minecraft's first new dimension in over 14 years. It's coming to Java and Bedrock in 2027, with no exact date yet. You can already visit it in Minecraft Dungeons II.

Ice Caves. The first look at the last game drop of 2026: a new cave biome, a frozen zombie, and a new Freezing effect. Full details.

Minecraft Dungeons II. The sequel to Minecraft Dungeons, released three days after the show on September 29, 2026.

Minecraft on Nintendo Switch 2. A native Switch 2 version, released October 27, 2026, with Vibrant Visuals on by default.

Sound Effects Packs. A new kind of Marketplace pack that changes the game's sounds.

Creator Platform. An official platform for making, managing, and sharing Minecraft content. No date given.

Minecraft World. The first Minecraft theme park land, at Chessington World of Adventures near London, opening in 2027. It includes Escape the Nether, the first Minecraft rollercoaster, and the first official Minecraft hotel.

Minecraft Live — May 30, 2026

A bonus show held in person at TwitchCon Rotterdam.

The third drop of 2026. The dappled forest and its camping gear, which shipped in September as Wilderness Bound.

A Minecraft Movie Squared. The sequel to A Minecraft Movie got its title. It's set to hit theaters July 23, 2027.
