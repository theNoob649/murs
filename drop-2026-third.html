Wilderness Bound

The Third Drop of 2026

  • Java: 26.3
  • Bedrock: 26.50
  • Released: September 15, 2026

Announced at the bonus Minecraft Live show on May 30, 2026, this drop went through the whole summer as just “Drop 3 of 2026.” Its name, Wilderness Bound, was finally revealed during MC Championship: Fall Stars, just days before release. It leans autumnal — a new forest biome, a new wood set, camping gear, and an unusually large pile of soft furnishings.

  • Dappled Forest: An autumn biome found near plains and cold biomes, full of poplar trees in red, orange, and yellow leaf variants, with coarse dirt, leaf litter, and a few spruce trees mixed in.
  • Poplar wood set: The full complement — logs, planks, stairs, slabs, doors, fences, boats.
  • Red shrubs and shelf mushrooms: New flora — the shelf mushrooms are bouncy.
  • Wool stairs and slabs: In all 16 colors, a long-requested building addition.
  • Concrete stairs and slabs: Added late in testing, also in all 16 colors.
  • Cushions: Sittable blocks in 16 colors.
  • Straw bed: Lets you sleep through the night but does not set your spawn point.
  • Abandoned Camp: A new structure generating across several biomes, with loot chests.
  • Better maps: Abandoned camp maps for eight biomes (found in camp chests), plus new explorer maps leading to Ancient Cities, Mineshafts, Desert Pyramids, and Warm Ocean Ruins.

Announcement to release: about three and a half months. The first snapshot landed in late June, a week after Chaos Cubed shipped, and the finished drop was out by mid-September. Drops are now overlapping: one is in testing while the previous one is still brand new.
