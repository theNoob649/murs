The Fourth Drop of 2026

The Ice Caves Drop — Currently in Snapshots

  • Java: 26.4
  • Bedrock: 26.70
  • Status: Not yet released — expected December 2026

Everything below is unreleased. These features are currently in snapshots and previews. Mojang changes, delays, and occasionally cancels snapshot features before release.

Name: Mojang hasn't announced one yet. Its testing hub calls this the “Holiday Drop,” and the name is set to be revealed during MC Championship: Absolute Zero on November 15, 2026 — the same way Wilderness Bound's name was revealed at an MCC event.

Revealed at Minecraft Live on September 26, 2026, the last drop of the year goes underground and gets cold. Snapshot 7 (November 3) added what Mojang called the final gameplay features, so the list below should be close to complete.

  • Ice Caves: A rare new cave biome beneath cold biomes, with icicles hanging from the ceiling and glowing ice crystals on the floor.
  • Brittle ice and Crevasses: A new ice block that gives way, and a new cracked structure inside Ice Caves that's covered in it.
  • Frostbite: A frozen zombie variant that lives in the Ice Caves.
  • Ice balls: Dropped by Frostbites, or crafted. You can throw them, or brew them into the new potion.
  • Freezing effect: A new status effect, with Potions and Arrows of Freezing.
  • Packed ice bricks, chiseled packed ice, and snow bricks: New icy building blocks; snow bricks come with stairs, slabs, and walls.
  • Calcite block set: Calcite finally gets stairs, slabs, walls, and pillars, plus Polished Calcite and Polished Calcite Bricks.
  • Chiseled soulshale: A rare decorative block found in Ancient City chests.
  • Jukebox upgrade: Jukeboxes can now play playlists.
  • Music disc “Flurry.”
  • Smaller changes: A cold variant of Chaos Cubed's Sulfur Cube, Strongholds that can generate in Ice Caves, and Strays spawning in more frozen biomes.
  • Java only — Vulkan by default: The very first 26.4 snapshot switched Java's default renderer to Vulkan, after it was an experimental option in Chaos Cubed.
